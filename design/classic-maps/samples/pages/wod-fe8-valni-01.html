Valni Tower 1

This is a map where you face random monsters, and you need to go through the corresponding levels to continue advancing. More general information here.

Reinforcements:
None.

Possible treasure:
- There aren't any chest.

Dialogue:
- None

General strategy:
Defeat the strongest monsters first, such as Zombies. This stage is very easy, if you have very fast units and good movement you will win in a few turns. On the other hand, this place is one of the best to get extra experience by maintaining a defense on the stairs WITHOUT weapons and letting your low level character defeat the monsters from behind.
