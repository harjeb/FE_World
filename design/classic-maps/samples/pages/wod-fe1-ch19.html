Chapter 19: Divine Princess

Light blue squares show your units starting positions according to the list when starting the chapter. You can see enemy data by hovering over it. VA = Attack speed.
Villages, items and treasures
1. Pure Water.
2. Bishop Ring.
3. 30.000 Coins.
4. Bolganone.
5. Earth Orb.
6. Seraph Robe.
7. Dragon Whip.
8. Speed Ring.
9. Star Orb.
- Orion Bolt (Sniper).
- Silver Sword (Hero).
- Light Orb (Thief next to the Throne).

Reinforcements
- None.

New characters
- Tiki (talk with Bantu).

Strategy
As you will see, this is a small map with many treasures. Enemy thieves don't have much of a chance to grab all the items, plus they won't move unless they're going to attack you if you're close to them. But at least ensure you get the treasure number 9 and neither forget to get the Light orb, they will help you A LOT.

Make sure to bring Bantu, the rest is your choice. But if you have one of the Pegasus Knight, an archer and one of your magical characters in level 20 or near, take them too, here you will find the necessary items to promote them.
Of the Pegasus Knight, I would recommend you bring Shiida or the sisters, and Merric or Linde from the mages. By promoting they gain more movement and the mages the ability to use staves, they can be a good help for you.

Enemies can be defeated one by one, or by attracting them with a character that you know that can resist (if they are against mages, use the Barrier staff or Pure Waters) and then defeat them, is simple. Just pay attention to the enemies with Killer Swords and the thief on the left with a Devil Sword, hit them from afar and then defeat them up close.

When you have taken all the treasures, you only have the enemies near the boss, who is Tiki. If you talk to her with Bantu he will manage to remove her from her state and will join you. I would not recommend that you kill her, as she will only give you a little experience and Tiki is much better than Bantu, she has better stats and her attack is strong against Fire Dragons (and almost always hit them, unlike Bantu who miss often).
