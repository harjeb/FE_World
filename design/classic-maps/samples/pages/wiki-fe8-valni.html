The Tower of Valni (Japanese: ヴェルニの塔 Tower of Velni) is a map location in Fire Emblem: The Sacred Stones that is unlocked after completing Chapter 8. The tower consists of eight different floors in which the player fights against various monsters. Completing the tower during the Creature Campaign unlocks five bonus characters: Caellach, Orson, Riev, Ismaire, and Selena.

On higher floors of the tower, there are chests with D-level items and gold, which tends to make up for the broken weapons lost during battles. In addition, the last boss, a Cyclops, has a chance to drop a valuable stat boosting item or a Master Seal.

Trivia

  • If one looks through The Sacred Stones text files, there are text labels for a ninth and tenth floor of the Tower of Valni.
  • In the prototype of The Sacred Stones, the tower was called the バレアルの塔 Tower of Baleal.
