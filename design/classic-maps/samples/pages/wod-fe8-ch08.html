Chapter 8: It's a Trap!

Objective: Seize the throne

Items:
1. Angelic Robe
2. Silver Sword
3. Elysian Whip
A. Door Key (Knight)
- Lockpick (reinforcement Thief)
- 10.000 (at the end of the chapter)

Reinforcements:
- Turn 5: 3 Cavalier next to where Ephraim appears.
- When entering the green zone:
   2 Mage from above and a Thief from the stairs.

New characters:
- Ephraim, Kyle, Forde (starting turn 2).

Dialogue:
- Events:
Eirika → Ephraim (Talk).
Franz → Forde (Talk).
Seth → Kyle (Talk).

Strategy:
In this map you will find many enemies, try to finish them fast. There’s also many good items, use Colm to get them. Try to send your units through different paths so you can end this faster.

At the second turn, Ephraim, Forde and Kyle will appear from the south-west area, send each groups through different path, and reunite them near the boss. Once there, defeat the boss to finish this chapter.

Boss Data

This boss is harder than the others, watch out with his Silver Lance and more if you are using characters with low HP. The best way to defeat him is using a good mage (rescue him if is weak, so he doesn't attack him in the enemy turn) and attacking him with Ross and Garcia in the pillars to gain evasion, the rest is up to you.

After a short story, you’ll be able to choose for two different routes, one for Eirika, and one for Ephraim. Both routes are very different from each other: different Lord, different Items, different characters (only in their appearance order), the history, etc. Each one has 8 chapters and come together at Chapter 17. Ephraim’s is a little harder, good luck!

Route Division - Eirika or Ephraim Route

After a brief bit of dialogue, you'll be given the option to continue along one of two routes: Ephraim's or Eirika's. The routes are quite different, but after a few chapters, the division remains the same; there isn't anything unique to achieve or a different ending if you choose one or the other.
In both, the chapters, characters (only their order of appearance), Lords, and items vary, and a bit more of the story is revealed from Eirika's or Ephraim's perspective depending on your choice, as they also travel through different parts of the continent.
You can only choose one. Each route consists of eight chapters that merge at chapter 17, but Ephraim's is somewhat more difficult, especially if you haven't gained additional experience from optional battles. Good luck.
